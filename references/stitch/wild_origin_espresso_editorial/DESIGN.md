## Brand & Style
Rooted in the untamed earth of Yunlin and elevated through high-fashion editorial rigor, the design system channels an uncompromising tension between raw agricultural origin and ultra-refined haute couture packaging. The aesthetic synthesizes avant-garde magazine spreads with brutalist structural packaging grids. 

Visual density is deliberate, unyielding, and starkly architectural:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep roasted extraction, intense chiaroscuro, and artisanal precision.
- **Audience:** Discerning coffee connoisseurs, creative directors, and purveyors of contemporary craft who demand depth over generic minimalism.
- **Movement:** Brutalist Editorial Luxury. High-contrast typography drives the narrative, while strict 0-radius borders, dense vertical alignments, and hairline framework rules create the sensation of an unreleased art book or collectible packaging manual.

## Colors
The palette is built upon dark, roasted saturation punctured by the golden tension of espresso crema and high-visibility industrial accents.

- **Primary (`#18110D` - Espresso Carbon):** The absolute structural baseline. Deep, charred roast that absorbs light and acts as the deep canvas for layered typography and content cells.
- **Secondary (`#C88242` - Crema Amber):** The tonal bridge representing rich emulsion, dense foam, and warm metallic foils. Used for secondary highlights, monospaced indices, and refined hairline dividers.
- **Tertiary (`#FF5A1F` - Neon Tangerine):** A razor-sharp citrus shock evoking raw acidity, harvest origin markers, micro-lot stamps, and interactive focal points.
- **Neutral (`#F8F5F0` - Crisp Off-White):** Unbleached vellum paper. Delivers severe editorial contrast against `#18110D` for primary typography, dominant metadata, and packaging bounding boxes.

### Application Guidelines
- Surface tiers are constructed using stepped shades of carbon black mixed with rich raw umber: canvas base (`#18110D`), surface container (`#221A15`), and elevated container (`#2D231D`).
- Hairline rules (`1px`) should prioritize muted crema (`rgba(200, 130, 66, 0.25)`) or stark white lines (`rgba(248, 245, 240, 0.15)`) to carve distinct brutalist matrix cells across the dark canvas.

## Typography
Typography is the core architecture of the design system. The dynamic collision between the classical, high-contrast, razor-thin hairlines of **Bodoni Moda** and the clean, utilitarian structure of **Hanken Grotesk** establishes an immediate editorial tension.

### Guidelines
- **Bodoni Moda** is reserved exclusively for high-impact editorial statements, portfolio project indices, and large display titles. Always maintain tight negative tracking on display sizes to mirror traditional luxury fashion headlines.
- **Hanken Grotesk** governs functional content, technical specs (elevation, bean variety, roast profiles), and system controls.
- **Uppercase Discipline:** All `label-caps` and technical categorization metadata must be uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.1em` to `0.15em`) to evoke serial numbers and botanical packaging labels.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a brutalist, boundary-defined 12-column fluid grid system framed by structural perimeter rules. Content avoids soft floating buffers; instead, sections sit within edge-to-edge enclosing lines reminiscent of print broadsheets.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Behaviors
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Modules display in expansive horizontal alignments, with massive editorial title spans and asymmetrical packaging breakdowns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Content reflows into dual-column cards; large display titles scale down to avoid awkward hyphenations.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 0.75rem`. Cards and editorial panels stack strictly in a vertical packaging spine with visible full-width divider borders.

### Rhythmic Discipline
Spacers strictly modulate density:
- Compact groupings (labels, micro-specs, coffee notes) use `space-xs` to `space-sm`.
- Card internal paddings use `space-md` or `space-lg`.
- Major thematic transitions between portfolio projects utilize monumental rhythm via `space-2xl` and `space-3xl`.

## Elevation & Depth
In alignment with the sharp editorial print aesthetic, standard drop shadows and blurs are entirely rejected. Depth is achieved purely through **tonal stacking**, **opaque contrast shifts**, and **hairline matrix borders**.

### Structural Elevation Rules
1. **Level 0 (Foundation Canvas):** `#18110D` — Ground base representing raw espresso roast.
2. **Level 1 (Structural Panels & Modular Blocks):** `#221A15` delineated by a crisp `1px solid rgba(248, 245, 240, 0.12)` border.
3. **Level 2 (Active/Hover Layer & Overlays):** `#2D231D` paired with an accent perimeter of `1px solid #C88242` or a stark `#F8F5F0` hairline.
4. **Physical Offset ("Hard Shadow"):** When dramatic tactile separation is required for floating controls or highlighted portfolio plaques, use a hard 0-blur mechanical offset: `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px 0px #FF5A1F` or `4px 4px 0px 0px #C88242`.
5. **No Glassmorphism:** Never use frosted glass or alpha blurs. All overlays are 100% opaque or sheer high-contrast mono-tint overlays.

## Shapes
The shape philosophy is strictly **Sharp (`0`)**. Every element—from primary action triggers and modal dialogs to full-bleed image viewports and tag chips—features crisp, unyielding 90-degree right angles (`border-radius: 0px`).

This strict angularity mirrors heavy cardstock, guillotine-trimmed packaging boxes, and unyielding industrial print plates. Visual warmth is derived exclusively from the serif letterforms of Bodoni Moda and the rich amber tones, never from rounded corners.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#F8F5F0` background with `#18110D` text. Strictly uppercase `label-caps`, zero border-radius, minimum height 48px, horizontal padding `space-lg`. On hover: background shifts immediately to `#FF5A1F` with `#F8F5F0` text and a hard 2px offset border.
- **Secondary / Framing Trigger:** Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(248, 245, 240, 0.3)` border, `#F8F5F0` text. On hover: border color transitions to `#C88242` with an inner solid fill of `#221A15`.
- **Text Button / Editorial Link:** High-contrast underline using `1px solid #FF5A1F` offset by 4px, accompanied by a trailing cardinal index or right-angle glyph (`→`).

### Cards & Packaging Blocks
- **Portfolio Specimen Card:** Flat `#221A15` container with a continuous `1px solid rgba(248, 245, 240, 0.15)` border. Dividers between the image area, the coffee lineage, and the tasting notes are explicit hairline gridlines.
- **Header Badge:** Positioned in the top grid strip, featuring uppercase classification (`LOT 01 // DEEP ROAST`) in `label-code` format.

### Chips & Spec Badges
- **Form:** Sharp rectangular plaques with `1px solid #C88242`, background `#18110D`, and typography set in `label-caps` colored with `#C88242`.
- **Accent Indicator:** Solid `#FF5A1F` badge with stark `#18110D` bold typography for origin status, processing method (e.g., "ANAEROBIC NATURAL"), or roast strength.

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Rectangular block, `#18110D` background, `1px solid rgba(248, 245, 240, 0.2)` bounding outline. Zero curvature.
- **State Changes:** On focus, the bottom border shifts to a prominent `2px solid #FF5A1F`, and the label elevates into `label-code` in Crema Amber (`#C88242`).
- **Placeholder:** Dim tone `#F8F5F0` at 40% opacity, utilizing `body-md` in Hanken Grotesk.

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- **Form:** 18x18px exact square with `1px solid #F8F5F0`. No curves.
- **Checked State:** Infilled with `#FF5A1F` with a solid off-black geometric center mark or sharp inner square.

### Lists & Technical Tables
- **Editorial Ledger Format:** Full-bleed horizontal rows bordered by top and bottom hairlines (`1px solid rgba(200, 130, 66, 0.2)`).
- **Data Pairs:** Left column holds uppercase technical parameters (`ALTITUDE`, `VARIETAL`, `EXTRACTION RATIO`) in `label-caps`, right column aligns dynamic descriptions in Bodoni Moda (`headline-sm`) or Hanken Grotesk (`title-md`).

### Custom Domain Component: The Tasting Matrix Index
- A 5-quadrant or 5-bar brutalist index showcasing the five espresso dimensions (Acidity, Body, Bitterness, Sweetness, Crema Thickness). Rendered with stepped, solid block segments rather than soft radial charts, creating an industrial quality control readout.
